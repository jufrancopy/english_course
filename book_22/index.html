Las 6 Reglas de Oro para Aprobar el Book 22

1. Cláusulas con As if / As though (L1): Tras look, sound, act as if... SIEMPRE debe haber sujeto y verbo conjugado: "You look as if you are tired" (Quiz C #29). ¡Nunca omitas el sujeto!
2. Orden de Adjetivos DOSASCOMP (L2): La Edad siempre va antes del Color: "an old white house" (Quiz C #31) o "an old red car".
3. Reducción de Cláusulas Adjetivas (L2): "a car that has..." se reduce a "a car with automatic windows" (Quiz C #28). "everyone who is boarding" se reduce a "everyone boarding" (Quiz C #43).
4. Verbos de Elección (L2): Con choose, elect, designate, la persona va antes que el cargo: "chose Katie as her secretary" (Quiz C #26).
5. Reported Speech (L4): El Present Perfect del estilo directo cambia a Past Perfect: "I have seen that movie" → "He said that he had seen that movie" (Quiz C #32).
6. Partes del Rifle M16 (L4): Trigger: lo que se aprieta para disparar (Quiz C #25). Barrel: el cañón por donde viaja la bala. Magazine: cargador de cartuchos. Firing pin: percutor.
